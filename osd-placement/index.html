Overlay — anchor and offset, back in the visual editor

Interactive mockups for OpenIPC/majestic-webui#340. Nothing here is built yet — the point is to agree the shape before any of it reaches the source. Each round of feedback gets its own version so they stay comparable.

  • v1 — the proposal

    Anchor, both offsets and the proportional mode are controls again, and the drag writes into them rather than instead of them. The unit is yours: switch % to px and the number changes while the overlay stays put. Arrow keys nudge one step, shift for ten.

    They live in a panel that opens on the picture when you click an overlay, so nothing you need is below the fold on a laptop. Four more boards carry the reasoning: why the snapshot and fullscreen buttons moved off the video, what the same design does on a phone, and two directions for the round after this one — several overlays at once, and logos.
