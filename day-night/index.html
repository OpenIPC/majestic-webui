Day / Night — a page with 18 controls on it

Mockups for OpenIPC/majestic-webui#325. Nothing here is built yet — the point is to agree the shape before any of it reaches the source. Each round of feedback gets its own version so they stay comparable.

  • v1 — the section with almost nothing on it

    The reporter of #325 asked for two things: fewer words, and only the settings that are actually in use. Following that to its end takes most of the page away.

    • Three controls on the page instead of eighteen, plus the switch and its readout.
    • Three folds — tuning, camera light, older ways of switching — each stating its own condition rather than repeating a warning under every field.
    • The pin map is untouched. It is the part of this page that works.
    • A raw threshold appears only on a camera that already has one, shown with what it means on that sensor, and with a button out.
    • English and Russian, side by side — the section is fully localised so the Russian-speaking community can review the wording, not just the layout.
